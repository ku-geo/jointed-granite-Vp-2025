# Ultrasonic P-Wave Velocities in Granite Cores with an Inclined Joint Filled with Sand and Clay under Varying Water Contents

Experimental dataset used in the publication:

> **Karunarathne, P. & Lin, W.** (2025).
> *Ultrasonic P-wave velocities in granite cores with an inclined joint filled with sand and clay under varying water contents.*
> *International Journal of Rock Mechanics and Mining Sciences*, **195**, 106316.
> https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijrmms.2025.106316

## Overview

This study adopts an experimental approach to estimate ultrasonic P-wave
velocity in granite core samples containing inclined rock joints with
varying infilling materials and conditions. Intact granite cores were first
prepared with artificial discontinuity planes at six distinct inclination
angles. Ultrasonic P-wave velocity measurements were then conducted for
each jointed specimen using a pulse transmission technique.

Subsequently, different infilling materials were introduced under both dry
and saturated conditions across multiple experimental stages. For each
configuration (joint angle and infilling condition), P-wave velocities
were systematically measured. The measured velocities were normalized with
respect to the corresponding intact rock values, and the results were
analyzed as a function of joint inclination.

The observed variations in normalized velocity were evaluated in terms of
hydromechanical coupling effects, temporal influences, and the physical
characteristics of the infilling materials. Finally, the study discusses
the broader implications, potential applications, and inherent limitations
of the experimental findings.
